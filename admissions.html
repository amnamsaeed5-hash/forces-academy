Admission Process & Registration

Secure your seat today at Forces Academy Faisalabad. Fill out the form below or follow the simple guidelines to register.

Registration Policy

  • Form Submission: Fill out the online application accurately or visit the campus physically.
  • Documents Required: Bring 2 passport-size photographs and a copy of your previous academic certificates during physical verification.
  • Fee Structure: Admission fee must be submitted at the academy window within 3 days of registration approval.
Note: Seats are strictly limited for special entry-test preparation batches. Early registration is highly recommended.
